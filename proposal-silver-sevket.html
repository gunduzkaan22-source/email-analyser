Silver Sevket & Co — AI Invoice Processing Proposal
01  —  Executive Summary
The right choice for a regulated firm

Silver Sevket & Co is losing skilled bookkeeper hours to a data-entry task that can be reliably automated. This proposal delivers an AI-powered invoice processing system — built specifically for your QuickBooks environment, your VAT obligations, and your ICAEW compliance requirements — that extracts invoice data, presents it for human approval, and books confirmed transactions directly to QuickBooks. The target is your first live invoice processed within 8 weeks of engagement start, with full GDPR documentation in place from day one.

02  —  Problem Statement
Manual transcription is costing you more than time

Your staff currently read each incoming invoice manually, transcribe supplier name, net amount, VAT, and due date, then re-enter that data into QuickBooks by hand. Across two offices, this is 30–60 minutes of skilled bookkeeper time per day — time spent on data transcription, not accounting judgement.

The risk compounds invisibly. A misread VAT figure, a transposed due date, or a confused supplier name creates a downstream error that takes longer to fix than it took to make. For an ICAEW-regulated firm, an incorrect VAT posting that reaches an HMRC submission is not an inconvenience — it is a liability. Your current process also has no audit trail: there is no way to know how often errors occur, how long they persist, or who last touched a record.

Left unsolved, this problem scales with your client volume. Every new invoice is another manual task, another risk of error, and another opportunity for a delayed payment to trigger a supplier dispute.

03  —  Proposed Solution
Structured automation with a human approval gate

An AI invoice processing pipeline that eliminates manual transcription while keeping your staff firmly in control of what gets posted. Incoming PDF invoices are submitted through a secure web portal. Claude AI reads each invoice and extracts the four key fields: supplier name, amount, VAT, and due date. A bookkeeper then reviews each extraction alongside the original invoice in a side-by-side approval interface — with confidence indicators flagging any item the model is uncertain about. Only approved records are posted to QuickBooks. Nothing is ever booked automatically.

This is not a black box replacing your staff. It is a structured data pipeline that removes the transcription step and adds an auditable approval layer your current process lacks entirely. Your bookkeepers make faster, better-informed decisions — with full visibility into what the AI extracted and why.

04  —  Technical Approach
Built for your environment, not a generic template
AI Extraction — Anthropic Claude API
Invoice text is submitted to Claude via API. The model returns structured JSON: supplier, net amount, VAT amount, VAT code (T0/T1/T9/RC), and due date. Confidence scoring flags any extraction below threshold for mandatory secondary review — low-confidence items cannot be rubber-stamped.
Approval Interface
A web portal presents each pending invoice with extracted fields pre-filled and the original PDF visible alongside. Approvers see exactly what will be posted before confirming. Every approval is logged with timestamp and reviewer ID. Rejected items are queued for manual handling with notes.
QuickBooks Integration — OAuth 2.0
After approval, confirmed bill records are posted to QuickBooks Online via the Intuit API using OAuth 2.0. The integration maps to your existing chart of accounts and vendor list — account codes and vendor IDs are confirmed during Discovery before any live data is touched.
Infrastructure & Security
Hosted on EU-based cloud infrastructure (Supabase / Postgres, Ireland). Row Level Security enforced at database level. Session cookies: Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict. All data in transit over TLS 1.2+. Content Security Policy enforced; no third-party tracking.
Authentication & Access
Magic-link email authentication via Supabase Auth — no passwords to manage or reset. Access is invitation-only, scoped to approved email addresses. Each office can be granted separate user accounts with the same approval rights or differentiated by role.
UK GDPR Compliance
ICO registration applied (ref: C1928867). Anthropic DPA active — API data not retained for model training. Data flow diagram prepared. Analysis records auto-deleted after 90 days. GDPR rights implemented in-product: Access · Deletion · Export · Correction.
Scope boundaries agreed at Discovery (before any build begins). Supported invoice types at launch: digital PDF invoices from named suppliers on an agreed list. Explicitly excluded from initial scope: scanned or photographed invoices, credit notes, multi-currency invoices, reverse-charge VAT, invoices with embedded line-item tables. These can be added in subsequent phases.
