Dubai is a vibrant city located in the United Arab Emirates, known for its stunning modern architecture including the iconic Burj Khalifa, the world's tallest building. The city has transformed from a small trading port into a global hub for commerce, tourism, and luxury, attracting millions of visitors and residents from around the world each year. Dubai offers a unique blend of ultramodern amenities, pristine beaches, and traditional Arab culture, making it one of the most cosmopolitan and dynamic cities in the Middle East.
